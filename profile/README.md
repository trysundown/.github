# Sundown

<img src="https://trysundown.github.io/sundown-evening.webp" alt="A MacBook in bed late at night, its screen turned deep red by Sundown" width="320">

Sundown is a Mac menu bar app that warms and dims your display on a schedule, so the screen in the evening looks less like midday office lighting and more like the room you are sitting in.

The warmth control is a plain Kelvin ladder that runs from 6500 K down to 500 K in 100 K steps, which means you always know exactly where the screen is set rather than guessing at an abstract slider. Sundown runs on macOS 14 or later and is made by Jack Richards.
